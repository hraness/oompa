## Invariants

- Preserve one exact provider writer for a session, durable intent before dispatch, and generation-bound recovery without speculative replay.
- Keep credentials inside provider-owned profiles or protected local custody. Provider payloads, credential values and user transcripts are not validation artifacts.
- Separate ownership of the provider controller from containment of provider-created tools. A direct-child exit proof is not a namespace-wide cleanup proof.
- Keep hosted capacity, identity, migration and activation guards. No data erasure, quota increase, platform-check deletion or ad hoc process kill substitutes for a reviewed recovery path.
- Preserve stored identities and exact installer recovery evidence across the rename and platform work.
